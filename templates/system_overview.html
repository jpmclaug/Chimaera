System Architecture & Core Intelligence Engines

Clinical Stack

Chimaera is a high-density, multi-threaded market surveillance platform designed for Magic: The Gathering collectors, competitive players, and Commander deck architects. The application continuously scans public secondary market indices, regional local game store inventory, and retail outlets to detect price discrepancies, restock events, and arbitrage opportunities.

Scryfall Engine [ REST API ]

Provides sub-millisecond card name autocomplete, high-resolution scan art, card collector metadata, oracle rule texts, mana costs, and official TCGplayer Market USD price baselines.

Endpoint: /cards/named • /cards/collection
Mighty Meeple [ BINDERPOS ]

Direct integration with Mighty Meeple's Shopify and BinderPOS backend. Scans real-time stock levels, condition tiers (NM, LP), live retail prices, and complete trade-in buylist quotes.

Catalog: mightymeeple.com • portal.binderpos.com
eBay Surveillance [ BIN SCANNER ]

Monitors Buy-It-Now single card listings across eBay. Supports official eBay Finding/Browse API credentials with automatic fallback to high-speed HTML scraping for live bottom-dollar discovery.

Modes: Finding API • Web Scraping Fallback
MicroCenter #175 [ LOCAL STORE ]

Headless scraper utilizing Chrome TLS impersonation (curl_cffi) locked to Charlotte Store #175. Tracks sealed MTG boxes, precons, bundles, stock quantities, and Day-over-Day delta markdowns.

Store ID: 175 (Charlotte, NC) • Snapshot DB
Google Gemini AI [ GENERATIVE INTEL ]

Deep neural tactical analysis powered by Google Gemini (with automated cascading fallback across gemini-3.7-flash, 3.6-flash, 3.5-flash, and 3.5-flash-lite). Generates 1–10 card effectiveness scores, combos, power brackets, and upgrade matrices.

APScheduler & Daemon [ CADENCE ENGINE ]

Background scheduling daemon operating inside the web process or as an independent worker process (worker.py). Periodically polls prices every 6 to 12 hours without requiring browser interaction.

Discord Deal Dispatcher [ DISCORD WEBHOOKS ]

When any vendor's in-stock price drops at or below target threshold, Chimaera dispatches a rich Discord Webhook embed containing card artwork, target vs best live price, dollar/percent savings, and 1-click checkout URLs.

Tab-by-Tab Operations & Feature Manual

In-depth breakdown of user interfaces, data structures, and actionable workflows for every sector of the application.

SECTOR 01

Registry Dashboard (/)

Core Watchlist & Price Comparison Matrix

The Registry Dashboard serves as the central command center for personal MTG single acquisition. Users register individual cards, set target acquisition thresholds ($ USD), and monitor live price feeds across all three online vendors in a unified telemetry view.

// KEY FEATURES & CONTROLS:

1. Real-Time Telemetry HUD

Displays four top-line metrics: Monitored Targets (total cards in registry), Active Deals (cards currently at or below target price), Target Portfolio (cumulative target USD valuation), and Live Market Sum (lowest available in-stock market total).

2. Multi-Vendor Comparison Matrix

Every card renders a three-way vendor price box comparing TCGplayer Market Price, Mighty Meeple In-Stock Price & Condition, and eBay Lowest Buy-It-Now Price. The lowest in-stock vendor is automatically highlighted.

3. Tracking Scope ("Any Version" vs Specific Print)

When registering a card, collectors can select "Any Version" (evaluates the cheapest legal printing across all MTG history) or lock into a specific expansion set, collector number, and finish variant (nonfoil, foil, etched).

4. Tagging & Category Filtering

Assign cards to specific decks (e.g. "Atraxa Superfriends", "Modern Burn") or purposes ("Cube", "Trade Target"). Filter by tags using 1-click interactive pill buttons or the toolbar dropdown.

5. Three Interactive Display Layouts

Toggle seamlessly between Detailed Grid View (high-density cards with artwork and vendor matrix), Compact List View (spreadsheet-style rapid row triage), and Swipe Carousel View (focused one-card-at-a-time inspection for mobile devices).

6. Inline Surveillance Controls

Each card provides 1-click inline buttons: Poll (triggers immediate multi-vendor price re-scrape), Edit Target (opens modal to adjust threshold), Toggle MM Alert (enables/disables Mighty Meeple restock notifications), and Delete.

SECTOR 02

Priority Deals (/deals)

[ ACTIVE BUY SIGNALS ]

The Priority Deals tab filters out all passive surveillance targets to exclusively isolate actionable purchasing opportunities where a monitored card is currently in stock at or below the user's customized acquisition threshold.

// KEY FEATURES & CALCULATIONS:

Cumulative Delta Savings

Calculates total monetary savings across all active deals: $\sum (\text{Target Price} - \text{Lowest In-Stock Price})$. Highlighted prominently in the header HUD.

Discount Rating Badges

Cards display visual discount badges: Great Deal (≥20% below market), Good Deal (8–20% below market), or Fair Market (±5% of baseline), with exact dollar and percentage margins.

Direct Checkout Links

Instant 1-click external checkout links navigating directly to the specific TCGplayer product page, Mighty Meeple Shopify cart/product URL, or eBay Buy-It-Now search.

SECTOR 03

Mighty Meeple Buylist Scanner (/buylist)

[ BINDERPOS LIVE VALUATION ]

The Buylist Scanner connects directly to Mighty Meeple's BinderPOS purchasing engine. Rather than retail selling prices, this tool retrieves what the store is actively paying collectors in cash or store credit for singles.

// KEY WORKFLOWS & VALUATION RULES:

1. Dual Ingestion Modes (Single & Bulk)

Look up single cards with instant variant matching, or paste an entire manifest of card names (e.g. from an inventory export or decklist). The engine performs concurrent background lookups across BinderPOS.

2. Condition Tier Selection (LP Default)

Defaults to Lightly Played (LP) pricing, reflecting the realistic condition of played tournament staples. Users can toggle between Near Mint (NM), Lightly Played (LP), Moderately Played (MP), Heavily Played (HP), and Damaged (DMG).

3. Store Credit (+30–40% Bonus) vs Cash

BinderPOS offers bonus payouts for store credit over cash (typically +30% to +40%). The scanner defaults to Store Credit mode and allows side-by-side comparison with cash payouts.

4. Interactive Trade-In Batch Cart

Add quoted cards to a sliding Trade-In Batch Cart drawer. Adjust quantities, watch total trade value calculate dynamically, and export a finalized trade-in manifest ready for store submission.

MicroCenter Charlotte Surveillance (/microcenter)

[ STORE #175 • SEALED PRODUCT TELEMETRY ]

Commander Statistical Telemetry & EDH Analytics Deep-Dive

Exhaustive documentation of all mathematical calculations, card classification heuristics, gameplay relevance, and competitive target benchmarks.

EDH METRIC STANDARD

1. Nonland Average Mana Value (AMV) / Avg CMC

[ SPEED & TEMPO BENCHMARK ]

Mathematical Formula: $$\text{Nonland AMV} = \frac{\sum (\text{Converted Mana Cost} \times \text{Quantity for Nonland Spells})}{\sum \text{Quantity for Nonland Spells}}$$

Why Lands Are Excluded: Lands have a Converted Mana Cost (CMC) of 0. Including the ~36 lands in the average artificially suppresses the calculated value down to ~1.8–2.2, hiding the true casting cost of spells. Nonland AMV isolates the actual mana required to cast your spells.

Strategic Relevance to Commander (EDH): In a 4-player format, tempo and mana efficiency dictate win rate. A player with an AMV of 2.6 can reliably cast two spells per turn by Turn 4 or 5 ("double-spelling"), allowing them to proactively develop threats while holding up interaction. Conversely, a deck with an AMV of 3.8+ is forced to cast one spell per turn ("tapping out"), leaving them completely vulnerable to opponents' win attempts.

⚡ Fast / cEDH: ≤ 2.70 CMC
High velocity, combo-ready, frequent double-spelling by Turn 3–4.
🛡️ Optimized Midrange: 2.71 – 3.30 CMC
Balanced engine gameplay; reliable curve-out with strong late-game impact.
🐢 Heavy Battlecruiser: ≥ 3.31 CMC
Clunky early game; requires 12+ ramp pieces to avoid falling behind.

2. Mana Curve Distribution (CMC Histogram 0 to 7+)

[ PACING & CONSISTENCY ]

Definition: A histogram bucketing every nonland spell into its exact casting cost: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7+.

Strategic Relevance to Commander: Visualizes the deck's turn-by-turn trajectory. A healthy Commander mana curve forms a Poisson distribution that peaks at 2 and 3 CMC. This guarantees that your opening hand (average 7 cards) will consistently contain Turn 1, 2, and 3 plays. If the curve peaks at 4 or 5, opening hands frequently contain unplayable high-cost cards, leading to mulligans and passivity during the most critical setup turns.

Optimal Target Profile: 1-drops: 8–14 • 2-drops: 16–24 • 3-drops: 14–20 • 4-drops: 8–12 • 5-drops: 4–8 • 6+-drops: 2–6.

3. Mana Pip Demand vs. Mana Source Supply & Balance Ratios

[ COLOR CONSISTENCY & RADAR ]

How It Is Computed:

  • Pip Demand ($D_c$): Scans all mana cost strings for colored symbols: $\{W\}, \{U\}, \{B\}, \{R\}, \{G\}, \{C\}$. Weighted for split/hybrid pips (e.g. $\{W/U\}$ contributes 0.5 to W and 0.5 to U; Phyrexian pips $\{B/P\}$ contribute 1.0 to B). $\text{Demand \%} = (D_c / \text{Total Pips}) \times 100$.
  • Source Supply ($S_c$): Scans Scryfall produced_mana arrays, basic land types (Plains, Island, Swamp, Mountain, Forest, Wastes), any-color lands (Command Tower, City of Brass, Arcane Signet, Birds of Paradise), and oracle text mana abilities. $\text{Supply \%} = (S_c / \text{Total Sources}) \times 100$.
  • Balance Ratio: $R_c = \frac{\text{Source Supply \%}}{\text{Pip Demand \%}}$.

Status Designations:

✓ Balanced (0.80 – 1.20)
Mana sources closely mirror spell requirements. Optimal consistency.
▲ Surplus (> 1.20)
More sources producing this color than required. Risk of color flooding.
▼ Deficit (< 0.80)
Color drought danger! High pip demand without enough mana sources to cast on curve.

Strategic Relevance to Commander: Many players naively build 3-color or 4-color decks with an equal split of basic lands (e.g. 10 Plains, 10 Islands, 10 Swamps). If 55% of the deck's spell pips are Blue (Counterspell, Rhystic Study, Cyclonic Rift) and only 15% are White, the deck will constantly suffer from "color screw"—holding uncastable double-blue spells with White mana on the battlefield. Chimaera's Pip Radar and status cards immediately diagnose this flaw.

4. Velocity Metrics (Fast vs Standard Ramp & Tapland Penalty Index)

[ ACCELERATION & EFFICIENCY ]
A. Fast Ramp (CMC ≤ 2) vs Standard Ramp (CMC ≥ 3)

Fast Ramp (CMC ≤ 2): Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Talismans (Progress, Dominance), Signets, Llanowar Elves, Birds of Paradise, Nature's Lore, Three Visits, Wild Growth, Fellwar Stone.
Standard Ramp (CMC ≥ 3): Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, Skyshroud Claim, Gilded Lotus, Commander's Sphere.

Relevance: In modern Commander, the player who ramps on Turn 1 or Turn 2 casts their 4-CMC commander on Turn 3. Decks relying on 3-CMC ramp (Cultivate) cannot cast their commander until Turn 4, placing them a full rotation behind. High-power decks prioritize ≥8 fast ramp pieces.

B. Ramp Taxonomy (Dorks & Rocks, Land Fetch, Treasure)

Chimaera classifies ramp into three mechanical pillars:

  • Mana Dorks & Artifact Rocks: Highest early velocity; vulnerable to board wipes (Wrath of God, Vandalblast).
  • Land Fetch Spells: Puts basic or typed lands directly onto the battlefield; permanent ramp resilient against wipes.
  • Treasure Generators: One-shot explosive mana (Dockside Extortionist, Smothered Tithe, Professional Face-Breaker) used to surge into game-ending turns.
C. Tapland Penalty Index

$$\text{Tapland Penalty Index} = \left(\frac{\text{Unconditional Taplands}}{\text{Total Lands}}\right) \times 100$$

Evaluates lands that enter the battlefield unconditionally tapped (Guildgates, gainlands, scrylands, tapped duals) versus untapped/conditional lands (shocks, fetches, bond lands, pain lands, checks, basics).

Relevance: A tapped land represents a lost turn of mana development. If a deck has a 30% Tapland Penalty, the player will play off-curve on 1 out of every 3 turns. Optimized decks target < 10% tapland penalty.

5. Interaction & Removal Metrics

[ THREAT MANAGEMENT & PROTECTION ]
Targeted Removal Count

Single-target destruction, exile, bounce, and counterspells (Swords to Plowshares, Beast Within, Counterspell, Anguished Unmaking). Essential for stopping opposing game-winning combo pieces. Target: 8–12 cards.

Board Wipe Count

Mass removal spells that reset the battlefield (Wrath of God, Blasphemous Act, Toxic Deluge, Cyclonic Rift, Farewell). Used as emergency resets when opponents pull ahead on board state. Target: 2–4 cards.

Removal Mana Efficiency

Calculated as: $\frac{\sum \text{CMC of Targeted Removal}}{\text{Count of Targeted Removal}}$. A removal efficiency ≤ 2.2 CMC means you only need to leave 1–2 mana open on opponent turns. High CMC removal (4+ mana) forces you to pass turns without playing your own cards.

Instant Speed Ratio

Calculated as: $\left(\frac{\text{Instant Spells} + \text{Flash Spells}}{\text{Total Nonlands}}\right) \times 100$. In Commander, 75% of the game occurs during your three opponents' turns. High instant speed ratio (≥ 25–35%) lets you react with complete information before committing mana.

6. Card Advantage & Tutor Telemetry

[ RESOURCE SUSTAINABILITY ]

Card advantage prevents decks from "running out of gas" around Turn 5 or 6 after dumping their opening hands. Chimaera classifies card advantage into four distinct operational buckets:

Draw Engines

Permanents that draw cards repeatedly over multiple turns (Rhystic Study, Esper Sentinel, Beast Whisperer, The Great Henge, Phyrexian Arena). Target: ≥ 4–6 engines.

Burst Draw

Single spells that draw 2 to 5+ cards immediately (Harmonize, Fact or Fiction, Riskel, Windfall, Dig Through Time). Used for immediate hand refills.

Cantrips

Cheap 1-mana or 2-mana spells that replace themselves (Ponder, Preordain, Brainstorm, Opt). Sculpt hands and dig for specific card types without losing card parity.

General Tutors

Spells that search your library for any card or card type (Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Worldly Tutor, Enlightened Tutor). Drastically elevates consistency and power level.

7. Heuristic Archetypes, Gemini 1–10 Ratings & Power Brackets

[ STRATEGIC CLASSIFICATION ]
A. Automated Archetype Heuristics

Before calling external AI, Chimaera's local engine evaluates deck archetype mathematically:

  • Aggro / Fast Combo: $\text{Nonland AMV} < 2.70$, Fast Ramp $\ge 8$, and Instant Speed $\ge 25\%$.
  • Midrange / Engine: $2.70 \le \text{Nonland AMV} \le 3.40$, Draw Engines $\ge 5$, and balanced removal.
  • Battlecruiser / Big Mana: $\text{Nonland AMV} > 3.40$, Total Ramp $\ge 12$, and Board Wipes $\ge 3$.
  • Control / Interactive: Instant Speed $\ge 25\%$ or Total Removal $\ge 10$ cards.
B. Google Gemini 1.0 – 10.0 Card Effectiveness Ratings

Gemini analyzes each unique card specifically within the context of the commander's synergy and assigned decklist:

Core Staple (9.0–10.0)
Pillar card; absolute must-have for this specific commander.
Strong Synergizer (7.0–8.9)
High value; multiplies the commander's primary strategy.
Solid Role Player (5.0–6.9)
Functional filler; accomplishes a job but lacks ceiling.
Potential Cut (< 5.0)
Suboptimal; weak synergy, clunky curve, or clear upgrade candidate.
C. Power Brackets

Gemini classifies overall power into standardized community brackets: Casual (1–4) (precons, thematic brews), Focused (5–6) (upgraded precons, coherent strategy), Optimized (7–8) (high-efficiency ramp, tutors, fast win-cons, protection), and cEDH / High Power (9–10) (turn 1–3 combo potential, free counterspells, tier-1 fast mana).

System Operations, Automation & Discord Alerts

Automation
Surveillance Cadence & Background Worker

Configured via the Surveillance & Alerts modal in the tactical menu. Set the background scanning interval to 6, 8, or 12 hours.

In production deployments, Chimaera runs worker.py as an independent background daemon process defined in the Procfile. In local development on Windows, launch via start_worker.bat or start_worker.ps1.

Discord Deal Webhook Setup

Each user can configure their personal Discord Webhook URL in Settings:

  1. In Discord, open your server → Channel Settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook.
  2. Copy Webhook URL and paste into Chimaera's Settings modal.
  3. Click "Test Webhook" to send an immediate verification alert.
